## What we are / are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Custom AI systems around one real workflow | A digital product studio |
| Production systems in the client’s stack | An n8n / Zapier shop |
| A small senior team that ships | A bot platform (Yellow, Bolna, Leena) |
| 2–6 month engagements, then the next workflow | A 45-day chatbot install |

**Category:** custom AI for operational workflows.  
**Not the category:** generic “custom automation for mid and big companies.” Automation is how buyers describe the pain. The product they buy from us is the system that runs after off-the-shelf tools fail.

We can still *do* glue work inside a real engagement. We do not *market* it.

## Who we sell to

**Primary:** ops / COO / head of talent / sales ops / CTO at a mid-market company (roughly 50–2,000 people) with one painful, high-volume human process that SaaS and Zapier cannot absorb.

**Enterprise (Natsoft-scale):** proof, not the lead. “Shipped at 2,000-person scale” makes a 200-person buyer feel safe. We do not lead with “we serve big companies.”

**Not the buyer:** founders who want a pretty MVP, B2C apps, first-time Zapier jobs, “implement Yellow / Bolna for us.”

## How India is crowded (and where we sit)

Four layers. We do not live inside any of them.

1. **Product platforms** — Yellow.ai, Haptik, Leena AI, E42 (Pune), Bolna, Gnani, Skit. Buyer configures software. If they just need Hindi outbound calling, they buy Bolna, not us.  
2. **Applied AI giants** — Fractal, Quantiphi, Tiger Analytics. Fortune 500, 12–24 month programmes. We will not win that RFP.  
3. **Automation agencies** — n8n shops and “18 systems” menus (e.g. Adaptive Minds). Cheap, SMB, weeks not months. Saying “we do custom automation” puts us here.  
4. **Artisan software studios** — Sahaj / Thoughtworks-style. Senior craft, but they sell *software*, not “this operation now runs.” Our old site sounded like a junior version of this.

**Closest offer shape:** one workflow first, prove it, then the next (Kautilyan talks this way). We do not claim 45-day go-live. Our honest cycle is 2–6 months.

**Our slot:** the process is too specific for a platform, too serious for an n8n shop, and too small for Fractal.
