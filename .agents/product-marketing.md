# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-15

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** BitBlabs finds where work gets stuck in your operation, redesigns that workflow, and builds practical AI systems around it.
**What it does:** BitBlabs helps growing service businesses map one high-impact workflow, identify delays and handoffs, decide where AI and automation belong (and where people stay in control), then optionally implement the system. The first paid step is a 90-minute AI Workflow Strategy Session that produces a usable roadmap.
**Product category:** AI workflow consulting / operational AI systems (not a SaaS product)
**Product type:** Professional service (strategy session + optional implementation)
**Business model:** Paid strategy session ($2,000 / 90 minutes via TidyCal + PayPal). Fee credited toward a BitBlabs implementation started within 30 days. Implementation scoped separately.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Growing service businesses (~20–80 employees) where coordination, follow-ups, and handoffs still run through email, spreadsheets, and people chasing each other.
**Decision-makers:** Owners and operational leaders who own the messy process and feel the cost of delay.
**Primary use case:** One stuck workflow is leaking time/money (recruitment, sales follow-up, support triage, delivery status, internal reporting) and the buyer wants a practical plan before spending on tools or a full build.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Show us how work actually moves today and get a clear bottleneck diagnosis
- Leave with a future-state design and 30/60/90 roadmap they can run
- Optionally hire BitBlabs to build/deploy the system into existing tools
**Use cases:**
- Recruitment coordination (candidate → recruiter → interviewer handoffs)
- Lead intake / follow-up / CRM updates
- Support triage and repeated questions
- Service delivery status and client updates
- Recurring ops reporting and approvals

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Important work gets stuck between people, inboxes, spreadsheets, and software, even though the team is busy.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Buying another AI tool without knowing where it belongs
- Automating one task while the surrounding handoffs stay broken
- Free “AI consults” that stay vague and salesy
- Chatbot demos that don’t survive real exceptions and approvals
**What it costs them:** Delayed responses, repeated coordination, managers assembling updates by hand, growth that adds admin instead of leverage.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration that “we’re working hard but still chasing”; skepticism of AI hype; fear of paying for the wrong build.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** AI / automation consultancies and agencies. Often tool-first or slide-deck-first; weak on one-workflow depth and operational ownership.
**Secondary:** Off-the-shelf AI features inside CRM/helpdesk/HRIS. These fall short when the problem is cross-system handoffs and judgment gates.
**Indirect:** Hiring another coordinator / more manual process. Habit keeps them stuck; it doesn’t scale cleanly.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Starts with the business process, not a preselected AI tool
- Paid, focused 90-minute session on one workflow → usable roadmap (not a free pitch)
- Implementation optional; fee can credit toward a BitBlabs build within 30 days
- Designs human checkpoints deliberately; not “automate everything”
**How we do it differently:** Understand → prioritise → redesign → build around real owners, tools, and exceptions.
**Why that's better:** Buyer gets a practical plan they can run with BitBlabs, internal team, or another partner, without inventing a tool strategy first.
**Why customers choose us:** They want operational clarity and a working system path, not AI buzzwords.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Chasing updates, copy/paste between systems, growth = more coordination.
**Pull:** Clear bottleneck, future-state workflow, roadmap they can act on, optional build partner.
**Habit:** Spreadsheets + inbox as the “system of record”; another hire to chase work.
**Anxiety:** Paying for the wrong thing; another demo that doesn’t stick; disruption to current tools.
